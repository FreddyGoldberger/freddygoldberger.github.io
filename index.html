About

I'm an Engineering Manager and Electronics Engineer with a background in automation and instrumentation, as well as computer science. I was born in Bolivia and studied at the Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María in Chile, one of the country's most prestigious engineering schools. For my final project, I designed an IoT home automation system that optimized energy use for heating and cooling.

After graduating, I joined Modular Mining Systems, a Komatsu company, where I supported the Autonomous Haulage System in Calama, Chile. A promotion took me to the Global Support team at the company's headquarters in Tucson, and I later joined the Development team as Software Development Manager.

In 2024, I relocated to McLean, Virginia, to manage the Systems Engineering team at the Saxton Transportation Operations Laboratory. In 2026, I joined Capital One, and I'm currently completing a Master of Engineering Management at Johns Hopkins with a focus on Artificial Intelligence.
